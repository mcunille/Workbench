# AI disclosure

Workbench is designed and developed with substantial assistance from artificial intelligence (AI)
tools.

AI tools may be used throughout the project, including to:

- explore product and design ideas;
- draft and revise documentation;
- write and refactor source code;
- create tests and analyze test results; and
- review changes for correctness, quality, security, and consistency.

## Human direction and responsibility

AI assistance does not replace human direction or responsibility. Maintainers decide the project's
goals, requirements, and accepted changes. AI-assisted work is expected to be reviewed, tested, and
evaluated to the same standards as other work before it is accepted.

This is a project-level disclosure of Workbench's development process. It does not mean that every
line or contribution was generated by AI, and it is not intended to provide line-by-line provenance.
Contributors remain responsible for ensuring that their contributions are accurate, lawful, and
suitable for inclusion in the project.
